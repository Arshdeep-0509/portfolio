The platform takes one strategy — RSI-HA — and applies it the same way every time, so a decision
rests on rules rather than on how a chart looks that morning.

## Two timeframes, one set of rules

Intraday and swing trading need different chart intervals, but the logic stays the same: Heikin-Ashi
candles for the trend, RSI for momentum.

## A note on risk

The platform is an analysis tool, not financial advice.
