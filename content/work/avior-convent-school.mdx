The site is live at [aviorconventschool.com](https://aviorconventschool.com/Default.aspx). It
gives parents what they look for first: the school's affiliation, how admission works, what the
campus offers and how to reach the office.

## Built around what parents ask

The navigation follows the questions families bring to a school visit — about the school, admission,
achievements, facilities, public disclosures, galleries and contact — so each answer is one click
from the home page.
